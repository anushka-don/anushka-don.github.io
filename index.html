About

Using biology and data to understand diseases

I’m a MS Bioinformatics graduate from Boston University. Through research, coursework, and independent projects, I’ve worked in NGS data analysis, multi-omics integration, machine learning, and reproducible computational workflows.

I enjoy projects where there is a real biological question behind the data. I’m still early in my career, but I like learning by building, whether that means working through messy datasets, testing different analysis approaches, or turning results into something useful.

Through my past projects, I’ve realized that real-world biological data and questions related to disease prevention, early detection, and biomarker discovery are what excite me the most.
