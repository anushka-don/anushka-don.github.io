About

Curious about disease biology, grounded in data.

I’m an MS Bioinformatics graduate from Boston University with hands-on experience in NGS data analysis, multi-omics projects, machine learning, and research tools.

I enjoy projects where there is a real biological question behind the data. I’m still early in my career, but I like learning by building — working through messy datasets, testing different approaches, and turning results into something useful. I’m especially drawn to questions related to disease prevention, early detection, and biomarker discovery.
